# 4. Solution Strategy

_Part of [Yggdrasil — Architecture (arc42)](../README.md) · [§4](README.md)._

The strategy the system follows, summarized from the decisions logged in §9. Items 1–8 record decisions already in force (ADR-0001 – ADR-0008, `Kind: as-is`); items 9–11 summarize the decisions ADR-0009 – ADR-0014 that shape the architecture-documentation subsystem; item 12 summarizes ADR-0015 – ADR-0018, which record the Architecture workflow's single form and supersede the mode-, review-, and scaffold-specific clauses of ADR-0010 – ADR-0014; item 13 summarizes ADR-0019 – ADR-0022, which record the TDD workflow as a second stage-composed workflow and fix how code reaches a target project's history; item 14 summarizes ADR-0023, which records the TDD workflow's own entry points and supersedes the no-trigger clause of ADR-0019; item 15 summarizes ADR-0024, which records the persistence step's two narrow writes to files it already wrote — the correction scope for topic documents and the status-line supersession for records.

1. **One orchestrator, five role-bound specialists, hub-and-spoke** (ADR-0001). Odin alone holds the `task` permission and may reach exactly the five specialists; specialists cannot task each other and never reason about the user's Deliverable. Odin never performs specialist work and never reads artifact files — it acts on executive summaries and routes paths (`agents/odin-autonomous.md:6-19, 38-40, 72, 81`). Serves system goal 2 (role isolation).

2. **Mandatory independent review by a dedicated reviewer** (ADR-0002). Every Mimir and Brokk Subtask receives a dedicated Heimdall review; every Orchestration Task ends at a Final Review Gate in a fresh Heimdall session; a verdict is `PASS`, `PASS-WITH-NOTES`, or `BLOCKED` and is never re-reviewed; failed reviews follow a classification ladder capped at three fix rounds (`agents/odin-autonomous.md:247-289`, § Review & Quality Gates). Serves system goal 1 (independent verification).

3. **Least-privilege permission blocks as the only enforcement mechanism** (ADR-0003). Every agent frontmatter begins with `"*": deny` and allows tools per role, with `edit` scoped by path glob and `bash` by command pattern, including shell-escape guards on `git` (`agents/mimir.md:6-68`; `agents/brokk.md:6-64`). Serves system goals 2 and 1.

4. **Generated agent definitions with byte-identity validation** (ADR-0004). Odin's three modes are one shared body plus a mode-specific Communication Policy fragment; each subagent is a template head plus shared workspace/tooling/memory fragments plus a workflow tail; `validate.sh` and two smoke tests assert parity (`scripts/README.md:5-103`; `scripts/generate-subagents.sh:103-121`). Serves system goal 3 (reproducibility).

5. **Two stores with opposite lifecycles** (ADR-0005). Transient, gitignored Workfiles in `.yggdrasil-workspace/` for intra-task exchange, written by Mimir/Kvasir/Heimdall/Bragi and read-only for Brokk; persistent, git-tracked Memories in `.yggdrasil-memory/` written only by reviewed pipelines (`agents/odin-autonomous.md:74-90`; `agents/brokk.md:53-56, 89-91`). Serves system goals 4 and 2.

6. **Trigger-gated packaged workflows as Odin-prefixed skills** (ADR-0006, ADR-0023). Research, Architecture, TDD, Software Engineering, and Deliberation Council are each a one-line triggering verdict in Odin's prompt plus a full-mechanism `odin-*` skill loaded on invoke; a packaged workflow is exempt from the Kvasir Consultation Check and inherits the standing review rules (`agents/odin-autonomous.md:172-229`, § Workflows). Every `odin-*` workflow skill now has a trigger verdict of its own; two of them — the Architecture and TDD skills — are also loaded as stages of the Software Engineering workflow, by the same body and the same mechanics (`skills/tdd/odin-tdd-workflow/SKILL.md:28-29`, § When to Use). Serves system goals 1 and 5.

7. **Three Odin modes that differ only in Communication Policy** (ADR-0007). Autonomous, Guided, and Interactive share every orchestration rule; the mode fragment sets whether Odin asks, suggests, or auto-proceeds at each checkpoint — the TDD plan checkpoint and the TDD suggestion candidate among them (`scripts/README.md:11-16`; `agents/odin-autonomous.md:290-313`, § Communication Policy › Trigger Thresholds; `agents/odin-interactive.md:302, 307-308`). Serves the user-steering interest in §1.3.

8. **Memory governed by command-triggered, reviewed pipelines** (ADR-0008). Remember, Dream, and Forget are the only writers; every write is reviewed; Forget is confirmed; Memories are consulted as leads and verified against live sources before use (`agents/odin-autonomous.md:84-90`; `skills/memories/odin-memory-system/SKILL.md:24-61`). Serves system goals 4 and 1.

9. **One long-term-relevance test, stated once, owned by the author and checked mechanically at persistence** (ADR-0010, ADR-0012, ADR-0016). The test — content belongs in §1–§12 only if it stays true and useful after the triggering task completes — is written once in the drafting skill (`skills/architecture/kvasir-software-architecture/SKILL.md` § Boundaries), narrows what the context step gathers (`skills/architecture/mimir-architecture-context/SKILL.md:25`), and — because the draft receives no review — is enforced at the persistence review for its pattern-matchable subset: three greps under the persisted target, any hit blocking (`skills/architecture/heimdall-architecture-review/SKILL.md:93, 96`, items (g) and BLOCKED). Persisted sections cite only document-stable references — repository paths, section numbers, decision IDs, and requirement IDs defined in §1.1 — so nothing in them resolves against a workspace that will be gone. **Tactics per subsystem goal:** Durability — the test plus the reference rule plus the persistence greps; Clarity — typed omission markers (item 10) and a report that states the scope line beside the decisions; Consistency — one test text, one workflow form, zero scope exemptions; Usability — implementation-phase content is named by tell-tales and excluded from §5 and §9.

10. **A closed set of three typed omission markers** (ADR-0011). `_Omitted — not architecturally significant for this objective_` (scope), `_Omitted — no evidence in context Workfile_` (evidence), `_Not affected by this change_` (unaffected by this update). The drafter picks by document scope; the persistence step copies a scope or evidence marker verbatim into the section index and never writes the unaffected one, and the persistence review blocks when it finds that wording in the tree (`skills/architecture/heimdall-architecture-review/SKILL.md:90`, item (d)). The `Section scope:` header line lists the included and the omitted sections. Serves Clarity (goal 2) and Consistency (goal 4).

11. **Document-granular update deltas with explicit supersession, and a correction scope for one document** (ADR-0014, ADR-0024). In update-delta scope the layout map names only the topic documents the delta writes; a document of an included section that the map does not name is carried forward byte-identical, and a document the delta retires is named in a `→ superseded — <reason>` row. The persistence step replaces exactly the named documents and lists as superseded exactly the rows that say so (`skills/architecture/brokk-architecture-persistence/SKILL.md` § Workflow step 5). When a persisted document's Workfile source is corrected after persistence, a **correction** run — the brief carrying `Re-persist: <path>[, <path> …] — <reason>` — replaces exactly those topic documents at their ordinals, regenerates their indices, appends one `correction` Change Log row, and writes no record: a record is corrected only by a superseding record in a delta (`brokk-architecture-persistence/SKILL.md` § Workflow step 6; `heimdall-architecture-review/SKILL.md:87-88`, items (a) and (b)). Serves Maintainability (goal 3): a delta that changes one building block re-authors one document, and a correction touches one document without replaying the delta. Framework skill changes land in the repository's `skills/architecture/` tree and reach the runtime through `setup.sh` (ADR-0009).

12. **One Architecture workflow — mode-less, stage-composable, with an unreviewed draft and a layout-creating persistence step** (ADR-0015, ADR-0016, ADR-0017, ADR-0018). The workflow packages `[Context → Review] │ Draft │ Checkpoint │ Persist → Review │ Response` over five skills (`skills/architecture/odin-architecture-workflow/SKILL.md:10-16`). No mode is chosen: the objective fixes what the document holds, and the drafter's two-fact check of the target fixes `Document scope:` (`seed` | `update delta`) and `Records from:` (ADR-0015). The drafting step is the one node without a review, permanently; its gates are the ratification checkpoint and the persistence review, which re-derives the target's baseline state and blocks on disagreement with the header (ADR-0016; `:14, 55-57, 79`). The persistence step creates the twelve-folder layout when the target is absent and the header reads `seed` — no scaffold step and no template skill precede drafting (ADR-0017; `:55`; `skills/architecture/brokk-architecture-persistence/SKILL.md:42`). An enclosing workflow runs this one to completion as a stage — no request line, no result line; the Deliverable is workflow-fixed (ADR-0018; `:18-22, 29`; `skills/engineering/odin-engineering-workflow/SKILL.md:26, 45-47`). Serves Consistency (goal 4: one form) and Maintainability (goal 3: four dispatches when context is established, six when it is gathered, two when persistence is declined — `:81`); trades a review of the draft for the two gates named (system goal 1 — see §11 R3, R11).

13. **One TDD workflow — standalone and stage-composed by one body, with an unreviewed plan and history written only by a reviewed commit session** (ADR-0019, ADR-0020, ADR-0021, ADR-0022). The workflow packages `[Context → Review] │ Plan │ Checkpoint │ ( Package → Review )… │ [Integrate → Review] │ ( Commit → Review )… │ Response` over six skills (`skills/tdd/odin-tdd-workflow/SKILL.md:10, 16`). The Software Engineering workflow runs it to completion as its TDD stage, exactly as it runs the Architecture workflow — no request line, no result line, a `Deliverable:` fixed by the inner skill; one body serves both paths, and when staged the Response step is folded into the enclosing plan's — so the enclosing workflow is reduced to a shape verdict over analysis and architecture, two stage steps, and a Response (ADR-0019; `odin-tdd-workflow/SKILL.md:3, 29, 65`; `skills/engineering/odin-engineering-workflow/SKILL.md:12, 26, 33-51, 55-60`). **Judgment and mechanics have separate owners:** one unreviewed Kvasir plan step fixes the packages (Appendix B verbatim, or one derived package), the execution shape under the five parallel criteria, and the commit plan — batches, order, type, subject, branch — resolving exactly two repository facts and running no tests; its gates are the plan checkpoint and the reviews of every session that executes it (ADR-0021; `skills/tdd/kvasir-tdd-planning/SKILL.md:10-18`). Implementing sessions change the tree and never commit; a fresh Brokk commit session records one planned batch as one commit, strictly by its plan row and the gating reviews, after re-reading the verdict lines itself, and that commit is reviewed like every other Brokk Subtask under `Focus: commit` (ADR-0020; `odin-tdd-workflow/SKILL.md:12-14, 61, 87-88` — § Purpose, § Workflow step 6, § Quality Criteria; `skills/tdd/brokk-tdd-commit/SKILL.md:10-12, 60-69`; `skills/tdd/heimdall-tdd-review/SKILL.md:116-129`). Every commit is a reviewed green tree state: batch boundaries are fixed by five soundness rules, a `Phase: red` result is never one, the four commit types map from package kind in the commit skill alone, and commits land only on a `feature/`, `fix/`, or `refactor/` branch (ADR-0022; `kvasir-tdd-planning/SKILL.md:86-97`; `brokk-tdd-commit/SKILL.md:24-41`). Serves system goal 1 (a review gates every write to history, and the write itself is reviewed), goal 2 (planner, implementer, committer, reviewer are four sessions of three roles, none writing in another's medium), and goal 4 (committed path set = staged set = reviewed union); serves Consistency (goal 4 of the subsystem: two workflows compose by the same staging rule) at the price of one more dispatch per batch and a longer minimum run (§11 R22).

14. **The TDD workflow is a first-class entry point: its own command and check, split from the Engineering check by intent, with a standalone safety stop** (ADR-0023). `/yggdrasil/tdd` and the `TDD check: command=<yes/no>, explicit-request=<yes/no> → <invoke/skip/suggest>` verdict fire the TDD workflow directly (`commands/yggdrasil/tdd.md:1-13`; `agents/odin-autonomous.md:211-219`, § Workflows › TDD). The TDD and Engineering checks partition one request space by two symmetric rules stated in both sections: test-first language *without* requirements-analysis or architecture-before-implementation intent → TDD; the same language *with* that intent, or a change that introduces structure or splits into two or more packages → Engineering, which runs the TDD workflow as its implementation stage; the one-package testably-stated change is TDD's suggestion candidate, the structure-introducing change is Engineering's (`agents/odin-autonomous.md:217, 227`; `skills/engineering/odin-engineering-workflow/SKILL.md:30-31`). The workflow body is unchanged between the paths: `odin-tdd-workflow` names both entry paths in one § When to Use, and its § Workflow reads the path in two places only — where the Response lands (step 7 folds it into the enclosing plan's) and at the safety stop below (`odin-tdd-workflow/SKILL.md:28-29, 65`). The one path-dependent rule is the **standalone safety stop**: when the plan finds that more than one package is needed and no architecture Workfile exists, a staged run returns to the enclosing plan whose shape verdict was wrong, while a standalone run ends before any implementation dispatch with a Response naming `/yggdrasil/engineer` and nothing left in the tree (`odin-tdd-workflow/SKILL.md` § Workflow step 2 (`:44`), § Anti-Patterns (`:105`); `kvasir-tdd-planning/SKILL.md:25, 102, 126`). The cost model adds one dispatch for the standalone Response (`odin-tdd-workflow/SKILL.md:95`). Serves system goal 5 (a bounded test-first change costs no Engineering-shape ceremony) and subsystem Consistency (every packaged workflow now has a trigger of its own; exactly one check owns any request) and Maintainability (one body, two entry paths — the stage-composition clause of ADR-0019 is untouched).

15. **Two narrow writes to persisted files — a correction scope for topic documents, a status-line supersession for records — and nothing else in a record ever changes** (ADR-0024). The persistence step has three scopes — `seed`, `update delta`, and `correction` — the third entered only by a `Re-persist:` line in the brief, never by the header's `Document scope:`; it replaces exactly the named topic documents, refuses any path under the record directory, promotes nothing, writes no record, appends no §9 row, and regenerates only the affected indices and the top index (`skills/architecture/brokk-architecture-persistence/SKILL.md` § When to Use, § Workflow step 6, § Quality Criteria; `skills/architecture/heimdall-architecture-review/SKILL.md:87-88`). Inside a delta, a superseding record's Appendix C row `| ADR-NNNN (existing record) | → status: <text> | — |` flips the superseded record's `Status:` line — and that record's §9 `Status` cell, and nothing else — to `Superseded by ADR-MMMM[, …]` or `Accepted — superseded in part by ADR-MMMM[, …]`, only when every named record is ratified or already `Accepted` and names the superseded one under `Related`; the review diffs every pre-existing record against the baseline and blocks on any other change (`brokk-architecture-persistence/SKILL.md` § Workflow step 5; `kvasir-software-architecture/SKILL.md` § Layout Map rule 4, § Decision Record Format; `heimdall-architecture-review/SKILL.md:92, 96`, item (f) and BLOCKED). Serves Maintainability (goal 3: a one-document fix is a one-document write; a supersession is a one-line write), Clarity (goal 2: a superseded record says so on its second line), and Durability (goal 1: a record's substance is never rewritten and the status line only accumulates — a wrong record is answered by a later record).

**Cross-cutting tactic named by the doctrine:** forcing-function verdict lines. Odin must state a one-line recorded verdict before every consequential branch — `Deliverable:`, `Kvasir check:`, `Research check:`, `Architecture check:`, `TDD check:`, `Engineering check:`, `Deliberation check:` — so that a skipped step is visibly self-contradictory rather than silently omitted (`agents/odin-autonomous.md:119-123, 160, 185, 195, 205, 215, 225`). The Architecture workflow's own hand-off lines — `Context: skipped — <reason>`, `Section scope:`, `Verdict:`, `Ratification:` — are the same tactic applied inside one workflow (§5.2); the `Deliverable:` line is fixed by the workflow skill and recorded at Odin's top level (`skills/architecture/odin-architecture-workflow/SKILL.md:18-22, 37, 49-51`); the persistence step's `Re-persist:` line is the same tactic for a correction, and its manifest's `scope=` field echoes which of the three scopes ran (`skills/architecture/brokk-architecture-persistence/SKILL.md` § Workflow steps 6–7). The TDD workflow applies it again with `Engineering shape:` at the enclosing level and, inside the workflow, `Context: skipped — <reason>`, `TDD plan:`, `Package check:`, `Plan amended:`, the commit record's `Commit:` line, and `Verdict:` — each authored by exactly one skill and only recorded by Odin (`skills/engineering/odin-engineering-workflow/SKILL.md:38`; `skills/tdd/odin-tdd-workflow/SKILL.md:38, 42, 48` — § Workflow steps 1–3; `skills/tdd/kvasir-tdd-planning/SKILL.md:68-73`; `skills/tdd/brokk-tdd-commit/SKILL.md:47-54`).
