# 8.7 Scaffold the target, draft the Workfile, fill the scaffold (R5 — replaces the R2 "scaffold-then-fill")

_Part of [Architecture Workflow (Yggdrasil) — Architecture (arc42)](../README.md) · [§8](README.md)._

Three roles, two media, one shape. The **scaffolder** (Brokk, `brokk-arc42-template`) writes to the **target project**: the folder layout of 5.1.13 with generated indices at `Status: Scaffolded`, and reports shape, scope, and `next-adr` (I-5). The **drafter** (Kvasir, `kvasir-software-architecture`) writes to the **workspace**: one Workfile whose headings are the twelve-section shape and whose Appendix D maps every heading onto the scaffolded folders. The **persister** (Brokk, `brokk-architecture-persistence`) writes to the **target project** again: it copies the reviewed Workfile into the scaffolded folders exactly as Appendix D says, regenerates the indices, and promotes the status. No role writes in the other medium (C-16); no role loads another's skill; the hand-offs are the I-5 result line (scaffolder → drafter, via Odin) and the Workfile + Appendix D (drafter → persister, via review and ratification). Judgment — pruning, splitting, content — is the drafter's; structure and mechanics are Brokk's; the layout spec is the contract all three implement (ADR-0016, ADR-0019, ADR-0020).
