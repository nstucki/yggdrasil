# 11. Risks and Technical Debts

_Part of [Yggdrasil — Architecture (arc42)](../README.md) · [§11](README.md)._

Risks and debts as they stand, each with evidence, impact, likelihood, and the mitigation in place or an explicit note that none is. R1–R9 concern the framework as a whole; R10–R20 concern the architecture-documentation subsystem; R21–R25 concern the TDD-workflow subsystem; R26–R27 are added for this document's own citation fragility and for the intent-based trigger split. A closed item keeps its number and states what closed it.
